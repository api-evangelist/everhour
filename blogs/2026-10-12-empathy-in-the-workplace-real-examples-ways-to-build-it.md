Empathy in the workplace is the ability to understand other people’s perspectives and recognize their feelings, even when you don’t share their experience. It helps employees communicate more effectively, handle disagreements constructively, and work together with greater understanding. Workplace empathy isn’t just about being kind or offering emotional support.
